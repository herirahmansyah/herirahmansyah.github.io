## Grammar Choice

**Chaptered editorial** (not filmic one-shot)
- Reason: The brief says "ikuti urutan section situs yang SUDAH ADA" — respect existing structure
- Hard cuts between grounds (drift steps, not smooth interpolation)
- Each section is a distinct chapter, not a continuous worldflight
- No magnetic CTA (banned in this grammar) — use standard button

## Signature Move

**Scroll-driven progress trace rail** at bottom of viewport:
- Thin horizontal bar fixed at bottom, present throughout page
- Fills with accent color as each act completes
- Acts as both progress indicator and visual anchor
- Coded in page, not engine feature

## Build Decisions

- **Aesthetic family:** Bold cinematic (not premium-minimal)
- **World:** Low-key cinematic photographic (per worlds.md §1)
- **Nav:** Fixed minimal bar (wordmark + nav links + dark toggle)
- **Hero:** Full-bleed poster with kinetic headline (greet cue)
- **Close:** Pinned stage with spotlight, standard CTA button (no magnet per grammar)
- **Total span:** ~20vh across 8 acts (slightly over 14vh target due to content density)
